How I work

01

Learn the domain first

Customs declarations, naval radar displays, data tools, a prenatal monitoring wearable. Each time, I learned how the work is really done, and by whom, before I started designing.

02

Design for the unhappy path

Most products work when everything goes right. I spend more of my time on the other cases: the action that fails, the data that is missing, the step someone skips. That is where people decide whether they can trust a tool.

03

A reason for every decision

If I cannot say why something sits where it does, it moves or goes. Hierarchy, colour and wording all need a reason I could defend in a review.

04

User needs and business goals

A design is done when it works for the person using it and for the business paying for it. I keep both in the room, and when they pull in different directions, I make the trade-off visible instead of hiding it.
