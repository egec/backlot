    Data health, product work at Insider One

    Making broken customer data visible where marketers build their audiences

    Marketers build segments on customer data they can’t see. When that data breaks, the audience comes back smaller than expected and nothing says why. I designed one health check for every attribute and event, shown where the segment is built.

    01The problem

    The segment broke at the last step.

    The problem

    Data breaks in many ways: a source stops sending, a value arrives in the wrong type or format, volume drops or spikes. Nothing in the segment builder said so, and marketers found out from an audience far smaller than expected, after every other choice was made.

    Elsewhere: Braze and MoEngage explain data type mismatches in their help docs. I wanted the marketer to see the problem and keep going in the same flow, with no doc to look up and nothing that reads as an error.

    Feature 01

    Data health shown inside the attribute menu

    The problem now shows while the marketer picks an attribute, not when the segment fails. Hovering an attribute opens a card with its sources, its volume and, if something is wrong, what and where.

    What changed in review

    My first card showed data only when something was wrong. The PM asked for the ingested volume on every card, healthy or not, so marketers can check how much data an attribute has before they rely on it.

    00

    The key decision

    Warn, don’t block

    Blocking the attribute was the safer option for the data, and I chose against it. A block would throw away every choice the marketer had made, the same last-step failure we were fixing. Most issues are partial or temporary: one source fails while the rest is fine, an API stops for an hour and recovers, a volume spike comes from a real campaign. And the marketer can’t fix the data; the data team can.

    So the card warns, keeps the attribute usable and links to Data Validation. The marketer knows why the audience looks smaller before the estimate surprises them.
