What's next

How I would measure it, and what comes next

Measure

Rejection rounds per episode

The north star. Around it: upload completion time, time to publish and error rate for the editor; thumbnail click-through, its lift over the default, watch start rate and completion rate for the viewer.

Test

Test it with editors

I would test two things first: do the rejection categories cover what publishers actually send back, and do editors trust the split between fields they fill and fields the upload already filled.

Design

Design what I scoped out

Unpublishing, bulk metadata editing and notification routing are each a small product of their own, and each one touches the same loop.

Later

Let the system do more of the tagging

Automatic thumbnail generation and ML-based tagging would feed the taste clusters, with the same rule as the rest of Backlot: it suggests, a person decides.
