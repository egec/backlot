Every file sits under the content it belongs to

So a missing one shows as a gap, before a delivery is rejected.

  • Each film or episode shows how complete its files are (2 of 7), so a gap surfaces before a delivery is rejected
  • Two views: Content groups files under their film or episode, Files lists them all for bulk work. Language and Type are filters, not groupings
