Feature 01

A rejection is a list of reasons

The publisher approves an item, or sends it back with reasons the editor can act on one by one. Each reason is pinned to its field.

  • The publisher comments on the metadata row itself. Each comment becomes a reason that carries its field and a category
  • Flagged rows stay marked on the page, so the editor resolves them in place instead of searching
  • Reasons resolve one by one. Reject counts them, names who gets the item back, and asks for confirmation first
  • A flagged field can be unflagged, because publishers are sometimes wrong too
Cuts the loop at Deliver→Prepare→Review→Release
