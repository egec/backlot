Feature 05

AI flags, it never fills

Catch the rejection before the publisher does. A wrong auto-fill is an error nobody sees, so every flag waits for a person.

  • The AI learns from past rejection reasons. Every reason carries a field and a category, so they can be counted per team and per region
  • Rules and AI stay apart. A broken rule is a failed check in the Readiness panel, never a guess. The AI only raises judgement calls and says how sure it is
  • The AI writes nothing on its own. A flag opens its field, or gets dismissed with a reason that stays on record
Cuts the loop at Deliver→Prepare→Review→Release
